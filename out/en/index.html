How admissions are won

Enquiries qualified instantly, so hot leads gets locked in.

Step1

A parent reaches out

Our specially designed and tested form surfaces what matters most to each parent, and we extract any shortlist or research they've already done to feed directly into how they're scored.

Step2

Lawrence scores it

A profile is built from their answers and research, then scored 0-100 in real time and sorted into Hot (70-100), Warm (40-69), or Cold (0-39).

Step3

Qualified and booked

Hot leads book straight onto your calendar, and every lead is prioritised hot to cold, so you and your team know exactly who to follow up with first each day, helping you win more admissions.

Step4

Opportunity logged

Every follow-up is logged as an opportunity in our CRM dashboard, so you always know where each family stands and nothing slips through the cracks.
